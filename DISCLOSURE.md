# Disclosure

## Who wrote this, and the author's position

Tomáš Havránek, a professor of economics at Charles University, wrote this
tool. The author is also a member of the Grant Council (Grantová rada) of GA
UK, one of the bodies in the chain that decides which applications are funded.

This is not a GA UK or Charles University tool. Neither has reviewed or
endorsed it, and nothing in it should be cited as GA UK guidance.

## What follows from that

The tool never grades. It gives no score, no mark, no ranking and no estimate
of anyone's chance of being funded. The real assessment is written by the
opponents. A tool by a member of the Grant Council that pre-graded the
competition would be the wrong thing to publish, and a pessimistic number from
it could stop someone from applying who should.

The author sees nothing. There is no server, no account and no data
collection: the chatbot or agent you choose receives your text; the author does
not, and has no way of knowing who used the tool. The one exception is a short
feedback questionnaire linked from the page, run on the university's Microsoft
Forms. Answering is voluntary and anonymous: it needs no sign-in and asks
nothing that identifies you. Do not put an application, a draft or part of a
review into it, because the author sits on the Grant Council.

It is public and the same for everyone. No applicant gets a version that others
lack.

It is not for evaluation. The author does not use it in assessing
applications, and opponents, rapporteurs, board members and faculty officers
should not use it on applications under evaluation: assessment is
confidential.

The application asks whether AI was used in preparing it. The applicant guide
states that this information is not a criterion for assessing the project.
The author can promise nothing beyond what the guide says.

## The declaration of honour

Applicants declare that they prepared the application independently. This
tool's policy follows from that and is cautious: it points to problems in the
text you wrote, never writes any of it, and refuses when asked. What other
help from AI is acceptable is a matter for the university's and your faculty's
guidance. The same declaration says the project text may not be given to
anyone outside the team without the project leader's consent. Whether to paste
your draft into a chatbot is your decision; agree it with your leader first.

## Many applications, one tool

If many applications at one university are checked by the same tool, they may
start to look alike. The tool limits this by never supplying sentences,
templates or model sections. It points at the applicant's own words and says
what is missing, and the applicant writes the rest.
